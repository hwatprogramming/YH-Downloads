FAQ

Why does my phone warn me when I install it?

It's not from the Play Store, so Google Play Protect hasn't seen it before and says it may be unsafe. Tap More details, then Install anyway. If your phone asks to let your browser install apps, allow it. You can switch that off again afterwards.

Do I need YouTube Premium or an account?

No. The app doesn't sign in to anything.

What links work?
  • A song from YouTube or YT Music
  • A playlist, yours or anyone else's, as long as it isn't private
  • An album from YT Music, and its songs get their track numbers too

Private playlists and your Liked songs won't work, cause the app can't sign in to YouTube.

My phone has no music player. What do I use?

Not every phone comes with a music player of its own, and on some the music app is YT Music. First check if your phone's own player was uninstalled or turned off: look for it in your phone's app store, or in Settings under Apps. If there isn't one, install a music player from the Play Store. The app saves songs to your Music folder, so a music player finds them there.

What can it get to on my phone?

The internet and your notifications. It doesn't ask for your files, cause Android puts each song in the Music folder for it.

What quality are the songs?

128 kbps AAC, saved as .m4a. It's the audio YouTube streams, kept as it is, with no converting.

A download failed. What do I do?

It tries 3 more times by itself. If it still fails, tap Retry. If lots fail, tap Update download engine (yt-dlp).

What if I share a song I already have?

It asks if you want it again or want to skip it. A playlist just skips the songs you already have.

Do plain YouTube links work?

Yes, but YT Music links come out cleaner.

How do I get the newest version?

Tap Download for Android again. Yours is at the bottom of the app's Help.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

It isn't from the Microsoft Store, so Windows' SmartScreen hasn't seen it before and says Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Do I need YouTube Premium or an account?

No. The app doesn't sign in to anything.

What links work?
  • A song from YouTube or YT Music
  • A playlist, yours or anyone else's, as long as it isn't private
  • An album from YT Music, and its songs get their track numbers too

Private playlists and your Liked songs won't work, cause the app can't sign in to YouTube. If you paste a song from inside a mix, you get just that song.

Where do my songs go?

Into the folder you pick each time you click Download, with the title, artist, album and cover art filled in. It starts on the folder you used last time. The first time, that's your Music folder.

Why don't my songs show in Media Player's library?

Media Player's library only lists the folders it watches, and that's your Music folder unless you add more. The app starts on your Music folder, so songs saved there show up. If you picked another folder, open Music library in Media Player, click Add folder and pick it. Opening a song from its folder plays it either way.

Can I close the app?

Yes. If songs are still downloading, it asks first. Minimise keeps them going. Close stops them, and they carry on next time you open the app.

What quality are the songs?

128 kbps AAC, saved as .m4a. It's the audio YouTube streams, kept as it is, with no converting.

A download failed. What do I do?

It tries 3 more times by itself. If it still fails, click Retry. If lots fail, it asks to update the download engine. You can also update it yourself with the gold arrow icon at the top.

What if I paste a song I already have?

A playlist skips the songs already in the folder you pick and downloads the rest. A single song downloads again.

Do plain YouTube links work?

Yes, but YT Music links come out cleaner.

Is it different from the phone app?

For now it's the phone app made for Windows, so there's nothing in it that only a computer can do.

How do I get the newest version?

Click Download for Windows again. Yours is at the bottom of the app's Help.
