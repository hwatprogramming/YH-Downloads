FAQ · 9 questions

  • Why does my phone warn me when I install it?It's not from the Play Store, and Android warns about every app that isn't. Allowing it lets your browser install apps, and you can switch that off again afterwards.
  • Do I need YouTube Premium or an account?No. The app doesn't sign in to anything.
  • What links work?A song from YouTube or YT Music. A playlist, yours or anyone else's, as long as it isn't private. An album from YT Music, and its songs get their track numbers too. Private playlists and your Liked songs won't work, cause the app can't sign in to YouTube.
  • What can it get to on my phone?The internet and your notifications. It doesn't ask for your files, cause Android puts each song in the Music folder for it.
  • What quality are the songs?128 kbps AAC, saved as .m4a. It's the audio YouTube streams, kept as it is, with no converting.
  • A download failed. What do I do?It tries 3 more times by itself. If it still fails, tap Retry. If lots fail, tap Update download engine (yt-dlp). YouTube changes things now and then, and the download engine catches up with an update.
  • What if I share a song I already have?It asks if you want it again or want to skip it. A playlist just skips the songs you already have.
  • Do plain YouTube links work?Yes, but YT Music links come out cleaner. A YouTube video's title often has extras like "(Official Video)", and they end up in the song's name.
  • How do I get the newest version?Tap Download for Android again. The button always gets the newest version. Yours is at the bottom of the app's Help.
