# Feature map

Every feature in the app, grouped by the screen it lives on. Check this before
building anything that adds or changes UI. A new feature gets a place here first,
then gets built.

A map, not a plan. No order, no dates, no status. Keep it to one page.

[One `##` heading per screen, with its features listed under it.]

## Catalogue page

The site's home page. It's `README.md` today and is moving to a website. In the
site's own theme, not any app's.

- The site's name and one line on what the site is.
- Every app, each with its name, one line, its download button, and a way into its
  own page.

## App page

One per app, in that app's own theme.

- The header, in the site's theme: "YH Downloads" back to the catalogue, "All apps",
  and a chip per app in that app's own colours, the current one filled in. It stays
  at the top while you scroll.
- The app's page has:
  - the app's name
  - what the app does
  - its download link, with the file name and what it runs on
  - a picture of the app
  - Setup, as numbered steps. Left out when an app needs none.
  - How to use it, as numbered steps
  - What it's used for
  - What it isn't
  - FAQ, each question opening to its answer. Left out when there's nothing to ask.
